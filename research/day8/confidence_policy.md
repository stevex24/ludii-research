# Day 8 — Confidence Policy

## Purpose
Distinguish game identity, rules equivalence, and suitability of historical BGG ratings as exploratory labels.

## Confidence levels
- A (verified): Independent rules comparison establishes agreement on material mechanics and game identity; remaining uncertainties documented.
- B (high confidence): Core mechanics and identity agree, but secondary rules, options, or runtime behavior remain unresolved.
- C (uncertain): Title or metadata match without adequate independent rules comparison.
- D (mismatch): Wrong game or material rules differences. Exclude from ordinary matched analysis.

## Label policy
- A: Eligible for exploratory analysis and later validation, subject to rating limitations.
- B: Eligible only for clearly labeled exploratory sensitivity analysis.
- C: Exclude from initial modeling.
- D: Exclude from matched modeling.
- A BGG aggregate rating is a noisy historical preference proxy, not an intrinsic measure of fun.
- Multiple BGG editions of the same Ludii game are not independent observations.
- A candidate must never be promoted automatically from title similarity alone.
